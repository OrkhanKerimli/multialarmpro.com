Common Questions

Is MultiAlarm Pro free?

Yes, MultiAlarm Pro is free to download and use.

Can I really not dismiss the alarm without solving puzzles?

That's the point. When a puzzle alarm goes off, you must solve the math problems to dismiss it. There's no force-quit workaround. This is what makes it effective for heavy sleepers who turn off alarms in their sleep.

How hard are the math puzzles?

You choose the difficulty. Easy mode has simple addition/subtraction. Harder modes require more thought. You can also set multiple puzzles per alarm if you need extra assurance.

Will the alarm work if my phone is on silent?

Yes. Like the native iOS alarm, MultiAlarm Pro plays through the ringer regardless of your silent switch or Do Not Disturb settings.

Is it available on Android?

Currently, MultiAlarm Pro is iOS only. We're focused on making the best possible iPhone alarm app first.
